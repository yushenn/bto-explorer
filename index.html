    How this works & data sources
    • Most to least expensive uses the median resale price per m² in each town over the last 12 months of HDB resale data (), calculated in SQL.
    • BTO prices are the price ranges HDB published for BTO flats offered in each financial year (April to March), FY2008 to FY. Each year only covers the towns that had launches. "After grants" is the price less the AHG/SHG grants for eligible first-timer families, as published. Today's grants are different, so check HDB for current figures.
    • Mature / non-mature follows HDB's estate classification. BTO projects launched from October 2024 are classified Standard, Plus or Prime instead.
    • MRT/LRT stations are placed from LTA's station exit locations. Hawker centres come from NEA.
    • Town boundaries use URA's Master Plan 2019 planning areas. Central Area and Kallang/Whampoa span more than one planning area.

    Data from data.gov.sg (HDB, LTA, NEA, URA) under the Singapore Open Data Licence v1.0. Map tiles © OneMap / Singapore Land Authority. This is an independent student project, not financial advice. Always confirm with HDB.
