    About the data
    • Town ranking: the median resale price per m² in each town over the last 12 months (), calculated in SQL.
    • Blocks: HDB's list of residential blocks (completion year, storeys, homes, flat types), placed on the map using HDB's building locations. blocks are shown. Block prices are the resale transactions in the last 24 months.
    • BTO prices: the price ranges HDB published for BTO flats offered in each financial year, FY2008 to FY. Each year only covers the towns that had launches. "After grants" is the price less the AHG/SHG grants, as published. Today's grants are different.
    • Nearby: the nearest MRT station, and the schools, CHAS clinics, parks and hawker centres within 1 km of each block, are measured in a straight line, not along walking routes. School locations are looked up on OneMap from MOE's postal codes. MOE measures home-to-school distance for Primary 1 registration its own way, so use OneMap's school query for that.
    • Updates: the data is refreshed automatically every month from data.gov.sg.
    • Lease left is estimated from the year the lease started (or the completion year), assuming a 99-year lease.
    • Mature / non-mature follows HDB's estate classification. BTO projects launched from October 2024 are classified Standard, Plus or Prime instead.
    • Not included: future BTO projects. There's no open dataset of upcoming launches, so use the "Upcoming BTO launches" button to check HDB's Flat Portal.

    Sources: HDB, URA, LTA, NEA, MOE, MOH and NParks via data.gov.sg, with school locations from OneMap, under the Singapore Open Data Licence v1.0. Map © OneMap / Singapore Land Authority.
